## APPROVED — Bring imminent acceptance milestones into the shared Work Queue

**User-impact note:** A Swiss construction owner, contractor, project administrator, or authorized adviser opening the Work Queue must see an approaching two-year warranty or five-year limitation milestone at the moment they decide what needs attention. A real target user benefits because a fully documented Case no longer disappears from the action cockpit while a saved acceptance-based deadline is within 30 days. This is not incremental polish: it unifies two established legal workflows inside the governed shared triage surface without creating new write authority.

**Explicit engineering task:**

1. Add a forward-only migration that replaces `get_compliance_work_queue_snapshot` and includes only the additional `acceptance_date` Case field while preserving the existing owner-or-active-collaborator authorization boundary and narrow projections.
2. Reuse `buildCaseDeadlinePortfolio` to derive the nearest upcoming `warranty-2y` or `limitation-5y` milestone per Case; do not duplicate legal date arithmetic.
3. Include an otherwise ready active Case when its nearest acceptance milestone is due within 30 days. Treat 0–14 days as urgent and 15–30 days as warning; keep expired and immediate-notice work ahead of acceptance milestones and preserve deterministic sorting.
4. Expose the milestone kind, fixed date, days remaining, and an acceptance-specific next-action contract on the queue row.
5. Render localized milestone type/date/countdown/next-action details in both owner and read-only shared views. Keep the existing Case link owner-only.
6. Add focused migration-contract, domain priority/filtering/sorting, malformed-input, owner rendering, and shared-view rendering tests.

**Guardrails:** Do not change deadline arithmetic, the Deadlines portfolio or ICS export, Case status, checklists, notice logic, sharing grants/revocation, authorization semantics, collaborator mutation authority, notifications, assignments, acknowledgements, legal-advice copy, or active-monitoring claims.
