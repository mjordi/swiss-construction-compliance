## Approved

### APPROVED — Source-bound, non-sending defect-notice preview

**User-impact note:** The target user is a Swiss owner, contractor, project administrator, compliance lead, or adviser who has documented a defect and is reviewing the legal next action. The workflow moment is immediately after completing the notice-source facts and before legal approval or external delivery. A real user will notice and benefit because the Case will turn those persisted facts and existing dates into one coherent, reviewable draft instead of forcing manual copying into another tool. This is not incremental polish: it is the first user-visible legal-artifact phase of the repeatedly deferred notice workflow.

**Explicit engineering task:** On branch `pipeline/2026-08-08-notice-preview`, add a localized, read-only defect-notice preview to each Case. The preview must be unavailable when any persisted notice-source field is missing; when complete, it must use only persisted recipient name/address and defect statement plus the existing Case project, canton, contract date, discovery date, and notice deadline labels. It must visibly state that it is a draft, has not been sent, and requires review. Add focused complete/incomplete and source-bound rendering tests. Do not add persistence, PDF/download, send/delivery, signature, approval, legal-sufficiency claims, new deadline calculations, or changes to evidence/protocol/checklist behavior.

## Engineering result
