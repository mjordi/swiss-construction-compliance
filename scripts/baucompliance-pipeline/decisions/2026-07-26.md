## Result

Implemented on branch `pipeline/2026-07-26-case-chronology-csv`:

- Added a pure chronology CSV serializer that reuses the existing legal-milestone derivation and preserves its deterministic ordering.
- CSV snapshots include a UTF-8 BOM, generated-at timestamp, case/project/canton context, localized milestone labels, ISO dates, and linked-protocol source identifiers.
- Every CSV field is quoted and syntax-escaped; spreadsheet formula-leading values are neutralized before quoting.
