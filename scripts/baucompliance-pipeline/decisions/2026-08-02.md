## Approved

### APPROVED — Correct the dashboard deadline calculator's legal date basis

**User-impact note:** The target user is a Swiss owner, contractor, project administrator, or adviser calculating legal dates before sending a defect notice, setting reminders, or preparing a dossier. The current page derives the 2026 60-day notice deadline from acceptance despite the established product/legal contract requiring defect discovery and contract date to determine the regime. A real user will notice and benefit because the corrected calculator can change the deadline they rely on and export. This is not incremental polish; it removes false legal confidence from a core calculation workflow.

**Explicit engineering task:** On branch `pipeline/2026-08-02-correct-deadline-basis`, update the dashboard deadlines page to collect and sanitize contract, acceptance, and discovery dates; reuse `calculateRuegefrist` for regime and 60-day notice behavior; keep 2- and 5-year dates based on acceptance; show old-law immediate-notice guidance rather than a fixed 60-day result; preserve all independent valid URL inputs and reminder presets; and ensure shared links and ICS exports use the last calculated snapshot.
