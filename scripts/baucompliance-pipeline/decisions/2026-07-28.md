## Approved

### APPROVED — Explain protocol finalization consequences before the legal record is created

**User-impact note:** The target user is a Swiss contractor, owner, or project administrator at the final review immediately before creating a handover/defect protocol. The user will see whether finalization creates a finalized protocol only or also updates linked-case defect-documentation readiness, and that the PDF becomes available afterward. This is not merely incremental polish: it explains the concrete legal-record and cross-workflow side effects before a consequential action, on a surface not touched by open PR #174.

**Explicit engineering task:** On `pipeline/2026-07-28-protocol-readiness-impact`, extend the existing final-review panel in `app/dashboard/page.tsx` with a localized, read-only finalization-consequence summary for protocol status, linked-case impact (linked versus standalone), and post-finalization PDF availability. Add translation keys in all four locale dictionaries and focused linked/standalone regression coverage in `__tests__/dashboard-linked-case-retry.test.tsx`. Do not change persistence, checklist normalization, PDF schema, routing, deadline math, finalization locks, or protocol save behavior.

This is visible legal-workflow guidance, not internal cleanup, and no persistence, PDF, routing, deadline, locking, or finalization behavior changed.
