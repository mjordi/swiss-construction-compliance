## Decisions

### APPROVED — M: Record immutable, source-bound notice dispatch

**User-impact note:** A Swiss construction owner or project lead, immediately after sending a saved notice revision, can record the exact revision, dispatch time, channel, and optional reference. The event remains retrievable in the Case’s audit history. This is not incremental polish: it closes the legally meaningful gap between preparing a notice and recording that action, while explicitly not claiming proof of receipt.

**Engineering task:** Add an owner-scoped append-only `case_notice_dispatches` record bound to a matching Case and immutable notice draft; implement strict payload normalization; add a narrow Case UI for recording dispatch; surface dispatch in readiness, timeline/chronology CSV, and audit dossier output; add DE/FR/IT/EN copy and focused tests. Exclude sending, receipt verification, carrier integrations, reminders, reviewer roles, and unrelated Case refactors.

### BACKLOG — L: Governed notice review and verified delivery lifecycle

High strategic value but unsafe for one daily PR. It needs a reviewer identity/authorization model, delivery provider and idempotency contract, receipt evidence, retries/returns, revocation, tenant isolation, privacy, and retention rules. The smallest credible prerequisite is today’s immutable source-bound dispatch event; a subsequent phase can add restricted reviewer access to one fixed revision.

### REJECTED — S: Link finalized protocol records to their Cases

Technically safe but removes only one navigation step on a surface merged today. A real user would notice it, but its compliance impact is weak compared with the dispatch gap, and approving it would underuse the demonstrated Medium throughput.
