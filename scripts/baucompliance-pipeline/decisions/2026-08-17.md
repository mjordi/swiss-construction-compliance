## Decisions

### APPROVED — M: Bind supporting evidence to an immutable notice dispatch

**User-impact note:** A Swiss construction owner or project lead, after recording dispatch of an immutable notice revision, can associate one existing private Case evidence file with that exact dispatch and retrieve the relationship in the Case and audit outputs. This is not incremental polish: it closes the provenance break between a recorded legal action and the supporting artifact while explicitly avoiding any claim that the file is independently verified proof of delivery or receipt.

**Engineering task:** Add an append-only owner-scoped association linking one `case_notice_dispatches` row to one matching `case_evidence` row; enforce dispatch/evidence/Case/owner consistency in the database; normalize the association in the domain layer; load and link existing evidence in the Case dispatch UI with duplicate-submit and stale-account protection; surface the user-linked filename and identifiers in timeline/chronology/audit dossier/PDF outputs; add DE/FR/IT/EN factual copy and focused migration/domain/UI/export tests. Exclude uploads, actual sending, carrier integrations, receipt verification, multiple evidence links, reviewer roles, and unrelated Case refactors.

### BACKLOG — L: Governed notice review and verified delivery lifecycle

Strategically compelling, but unsafe as the full daily implementation. Even the smallest reviewer-only phase needs explicit reviewer identity, scope, authorization/revocation, tenant isolation, immutable-decision, and evidence-access contracts. The credible phase-1 is one explicitly granted reviewer for one immutable revision with one approve/reject decision; it should be designed as a dedicated follow-up rather than combined with dispatch evidence.

### REJECTED — S: Make Protocol Register Case references actionable

Useful and safe, but it removes only one navigation step and offers weaker legal/workflow impact than a safe Medium provenance slice. Approving it would underuse the demonstrated throughput and repeat a class of incremental handoff polish that recent decisions deliberately deprioritized.
