## APPROVED — Track acceptance-based warranty and limitation milestones on saved Cases

**User-impact note:** A Swiss construction owner or project lead at the moment an acceptance date becomes known can save that explicit legal source fact on the Case, then see and export its upcoming two-year SIA and five-year OR milestones alongside any fixed notice deadline. A real target user benefits immediately because the accepted-work deadline register persists across sessions and no longer requires re-entering dates in a standalone calculator. This is not incremental polish: it turns an existing one-off calculation into a durable cross-workflow compliance portfolio.

**Explicit engineering task:**

1. Add a nullable, owner-entered `acceptance_date` to Cases and TypeScript types. Never infer it from protocol finalization or another system timestamp.
2. Add optional, labeled acceptance-date fields to Case create and inline edit; persist `null` when blank and preserve existing create/edit pending-state guards.
3. Extend the saved Case deadline portfolio to emit typed future milestones for eligible fixed notice deadlines, two-year SIA warranty dates, and five-year OR limitation dates. Keep deterministic ordering and collision-safe stable ICS UIDs.
4. Show the localized milestone type on each portfolio row and export localized event summaries/source context.
5. Add focused migration/type, Case create/edit persistence, pure portfolio/ICS, page integration, and locale parity tests.

**Guardrails:** Phase 1 only. Do not infer or backfill acceptance, alter notice calculations, add active notifications, reprioritize the work queue, change shared-queue authorization/snapshots, or imply that calendar exports are active monitoring.
