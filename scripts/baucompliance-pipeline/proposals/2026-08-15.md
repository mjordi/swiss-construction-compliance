# Product improvement proposals — 2026-08-15

## User and market lens

**Primary user/job:** A Swiss construction owner or project lead must turn a prepared defect notice into a defensible record showing what was dispatched, when, through which channel, and from which immutable revision.

**Workflow moment:** BauCompliance now helps users establish the notice source basis, preview a notice, save immutable revisions, and export a PDF. Trust breaks immediately after download: the product cannot record that an exact revision was dispatched, so the Case chronology and audit dossier stop before the legally consequential handoff.

**Best-in-market outcome:** A construction-compliance product should distinguish preparation, dispatch, and receipt; bind each event to immutable source evidence; and make the resulting history audit-ready without claiming that a user-entered dispatch record proves delivery.

## Small (S) — Link finalized protocol records to their Cases

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Project leads reviewing finalized protocols who need to continue work in the linked Case.
- **Problem:** The new Protocol Register exposes a linked Case identifier as text, requiring manual navigation.
- **Scope:** Render a native link to the owner-visible Case workflow using an existing stable Case identifier.
- **Expected impact:** Removes one navigation/search step.
- **User-visible outcome:** Users can continue from a finalized protocol to its linked Case.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Connected records should be navigable, but this is table stakes rather than a meaningful compliance capability.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** S.

## Medium (M) — Record immutable, source-bound notice dispatch

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Construction owners and project teams who must document that an exact saved defect-notice revision was dispatched.
- **Problem:** The product records notice preparation but not dispatch. A downloaded PDF can be sent later through a different channel with no timestamp, reference, revision binding, chronology event, or audit-dossier evidence.
- **Scope:** Add owner-scoped, append-only dispatch records linked to an immutable notice draft; capture dispatch time, channel, and optional tracking/reference; record dispatch from the Case notice workflow; expose the latest factual record in readiness/timeline/export surfaces. State explicitly that this is user-recorded dispatch and not proof of receipt.
- **Expected impact:** Closes the main trust gap between notice preparation and legally relevant action while preserving source provenance.
- **User-visible outcome:** After dispatching a notice, the user can record the exact revision, time, channel, and reference and later retrieve that event in the Case chronology/audit dossier.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Best-in-market compliance tooling should make source-bound legal actions auditable and should not blur dispatch with delivery confirmation.
- **Risk:** 5/10, mainly authorization, source consistency, timezone/date validation, and avoiding false proof-of-delivery claims.
- **Effort/size:** M.
- **Phase-1 slice:** One append-only record per dispatch action, owner-only Case UI, fixed channels, optional reference, timeline/chronology/audit output. Exclude actual sending, receipt evidence, carrier integration, reminders, external reviewers, and team roles.

## Large / strategic (L) — Governed notice review and verified delivery lifecycle

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Multi-party construction teams that need four-eyes review, authorized release, tracked delivery, and receipt evidence for defect notices.
- **Problem:** Preparation, approval authority, delivery attempts, receipt evidence, and exceptions are not governed end-to-end.
- **Scope:** Identity-bound reviewer grants, immutable approve/reject events, provider-backed delivery, idempotent retries, receipt/return evidence, revocation, retention, and tenant-safe audit history.
- **Expected impact:** Turns notice drafting into a complete controlled legal workflow.
- **User-visible outcome:** Teams can prove who reviewed and released which revision, how it was delivered, and what receipt evidence exists.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** This is the strategic workflow that separates an audit-ready compliance product from a document generator.
- **Risk:** 9/10 due to authorization, external delivery providers, privacy/retention, tenancy, retry semantics, and legal copy.
- **Effort/size:** L.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Today’s Medium immutable dispatch record establishes the event/source model without pretending to solve review authority or verified receipt.
