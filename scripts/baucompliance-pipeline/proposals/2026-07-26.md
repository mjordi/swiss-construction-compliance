# Daily Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-07-26

## User and market lens

**Primary user/job-to-be-done:** Swiss construction owners, contractors, project administrators, and their legal advisers need to reconstruct a defect case chronologically before issuing notice, escalating a dispute, or assembling an audit dossier.

**Workflow moment:** The user can now inspect contract, discovery, deadline, and finalized-protocol milestones inside expanded Case details, but loses portability and confidence when that chronology must be reviewed outside BauCompliance. A best-in-market product should turn the trusted in-product chronology into an obvious, deterministic, audit-ready handoff rather than requiring screenshots or manual transcription.

## S — Add a case-tracking-started milestone

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Project administrators comparing when a defect was discovered with when formal tracking began.
- **Problem:** The timeline does not show when the Case record itself was created.
- **Scope:** Retain the existing case creation timestamp and render one localized `Case tracking started` milestone.
- **Expected impact:** Makes intake delay visible during chronology review.
- **User-visible outcome:** One additional dated milestone in expanded Case details.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Better provenance than a chronology containing only domain dates, but still an incremental timeline addition.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** Small (S).
- **Phase-1 slice:** The proposal is already the smallest slice.

## M — Export a case chronology snapshot for legal review

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Owners, contractors, project administrators, and legal advisers preparing a defect dossier for notice, escalation, or external review.
- **Problem:** The cross-workflow chronology is useful only while viewing expanded Case details; users cannot preserve a dated snapshot or hand it to an adviser without manual transcription.
- **Scope:** Build a deterministic UTF-8 CSV from the existing legal-milestone contract, including snapshot generation time, case/project/canton context, ordered milestone dates and labels, and linked-protocol source identifiers. Add a localized download action and focused serializer/UI coverage.
- **Expected impact:** Converts recent timeline work into a portable legal-review artifact and reduces transcription errors.
- **User-visible outcome:** A one-click chronology download from each expanded Case.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** A best-in-market compliance product should make its audit chronology portable and reviewable, not trap it in a dashboard.
- **Risk:** 4/10, mainly CSV escaping/localization, stable ordering, and avoiding any implication of legal certification.
- **Effort/size:** Medium (M), bounded to a read-only export.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Deterministic CSV snapshot only; defer branded PDF, signatures, hashes, attachments, actor attribution, and persisted export history.

## L — Persist an append-only cross-workflow audit ledger

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Compliance leads proving who did what, when, and against which case evidence throughout a dispute lifecycle.
- **Problem:** Current checklist values and record timestamps are mutable and cannot provide an attributable activity history across cases, protocols, evidence, reminders, and archive actions.
- **Scope:** Add an RLS-protected event model with actor, event type, timestamp, source artifact, metadata, integrity/version fields, idempotent writes, filtering, and dossier export.
- **Expected impact:** Establishes the defensible audit backbone for the product.
- **User-visible outcome:** A unified, attributable chronology across compliance workflows.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** This is the strategic north star for audit-ready construction compliance rather than a collection of disconnected status views.
- **Risk:** 8/10 due to migrations, privacy, authorization, retention, backfill, retries/idempotency, and integrity guarantees.
- **Effort/size:** Large / strategic (L).
- **Phase-1 slice:** Specify the event contract and persist one idempotent `protocol.finalized` event while retaining source-record-derived milestones as the compatibility path.
