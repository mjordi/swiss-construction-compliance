# Daily Product Proposals — 2026-08-31

## User and market lens

**Primary user/job:** A Swiss construction owner or project lead must see which legal-compliance action is next, then safely involve a trusted project colleague without surrendering mutation authority or losing an audit trail.

**Workflow moment:** The owner has triaged deadlines, evidence, notices, and protocols in the compliance work queue, but the queue is owner-only. A site manager, Bauherrenvertreter, or legal adviser cannot independently review the same prioritized facts. The owner must copy information into email or meetings, losing time, freshness, and accountability.

**Best-product outcome:** A best-in-market Swiss construction-compliance product should make the current legal work state safely shareable, tenant-isolated, revocable, and audit-ready before adding assignments, approvals, or automated delivery.

## Small (S) — Explain each work-queue deadline basis in place

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Owners triaging the work queue who need to understand why a Case is urgent without opening it.
- **Problem:** Queue rows show status and countdown but not the governing discovery date / 60-day basis, so users must open the Case to verify the legal clock.
- **Scope:** Add a compact localized deadline-basis line to each eligible queue row using the existing timeline contract; show the immediate-notice regime when no 60-day deadline applies.
- **Expected impact:** Faster verification and fewer context switches during triage.
- **User-visible outcome:** Every queue row explains the date basis behind its priority.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Legal urgency should be explainable, not merely color-coded.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** Small.

## Medium (M) — Track acceptance-based warranty and limitation deadlines on saved Cases

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Owners managing accepted construction work who need one operational deadline portfolio rather than a separate calculator.
- **Problem:** The standalone deadline calculator derives SIA two-year and five-year limitation dates, but saved Cases have no acceptance date and the portfolio tracks only the 60-day notice clock.
- **Scope:** Add a nullable acceptance date to Cases, expose it in create/edit, and include existing acceptance-derived deadlines in the saved deadline portfolio and calendar export when present.
- **Expected impact:** Consolidates more of the post-acceptance legal calendar into the product’s persistent workflow.
- **User-visible outcome:** A saved Case can show and export its notice, warranty, and limitation milestones together.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** A leading product should connect legal calculations to durable project records instead of leaving them as one-off tools.
- **Risk:** 5/10.
- **Effort/size:** Medium.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Nullable acceptance date plus read-only portfolio/ICS inclusion; no backfill, notification delivery, or work-queue reprioritization.

## Large / strategic (L) — Governed shared compliance queue

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Owners who need a trusted project colleague or adviser to review the same prioritized compliance facts without being able to alter legal records.
- **Problem:** The newly established owner queue and exact-Case identity stop at one account. Collaboration happens outside the system, where data becomes stale and access cannot be revoked or audited.
- **Scope:** Build a governed collaboration foundation for the queue, then later add roles, assignments, review decisions, and notifications.
- **Expected impact:** Moves BauCompliance from a personal dashboard toward a multi-party compliance operating system while preserving owner authority.
- **User-visible outcome:** An owner grants one existing account read-only access by email; the collaborator can select and review that owner’s queue; the owner can revoke access.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Construction compliance is multi-party. Tenant-isolated, revocable, audited access is a prerequisite for trustworthy team workflows and a stronger market position than exporting screenshots or spreadsheets.
- **Risk:** 7/10.
- **Effort/size:** Large / strategic.
- **Phase-1 slice:** One owner and existing-account collaborator; read-only queue snapshots; database-enforced authorization; grant/revoke audit facts; explicit owner context in the UI. Exclude invitations, generic roles, assignments, mutations, evidence/document sharing, approval decisions, notifications, and delivery providers.
