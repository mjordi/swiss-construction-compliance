# Daily Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-07-29

## User and market lens

**Primary user/job-to-be-done:** Swiss owners, contractors, project administrators, and advisers finalizing and downloading a handover/defect protocol need a portable record whose claims can be traced to information they actually entered.

**Workflow moment:** Immediately after protocol finalization, the user downloads the PDF for handoff or dossier retention. The current report hard-codes a `98%` score, `Passed 12/12 Mandatory Checks`, and `COMPLIANT` legal checks regardless of protocol content. That creates false legal confidence at the most consequential export moment.

**Best-in-market outcome:** A Swiss construction-compliance product should never manufacture an audit result. Its exported protocol should distinguish captured facts (project, parties, defect/no-defect statement, signature, case link, finalization) from legal advice, and every visible status should be derived from the current record.

## S — Remove the unsupported compliance score from the protocol PDF

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A user downloading a finalized protocol who needs an honest artifact for the dossier.
- **Problem:** The PDF displays a fixed `98%` and `12/12` result even though no twelve-check audit is performed.
- **Scope:** Remove the score block and fixed compliance claims; retain existing metadata and disclaimer.
- **Expected impact:** Eliminates the most prominent unsupported legal-trust signal.
- **User-visible outcome:** The downloaded PDF no longer claims a fabricated audit score.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Trustworthy compliance software must prefer an explicit absence of assessment over an invented result.
- **Risk (1-10):** 2
- **Effort/size:** S

## M — Replace the fabricated audit PDF with an evidence-derived protocol record

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Swiss owner, contractor, project administrator, or adviser preserving and handing off a finalized construction protocol.
- **Problem:** The current `Compliance Audit` PDF combines real project metadata with fixed `98%`, `12/12`, `COMPLIANT`, and `RECOMMENDATION` claims that are unrelated to captured form state.
- **Scope:** Define a small typed protocol-report summary derived from actual wizard state; pass defect/no-defect and signature facts into the PDF; replace the score and canned legal-check rows with factual finalized-protocol fields; keep the existing legal-advice disclaimer and download flow.
- **Expected impact:** Converts a legally risky placeholder export into a defensible record of what the user actually captured.
- **User-visible outcome:** The downloaded PDF shows project/party/case context, finalization state, defect statement, and signature capture state without fabricated compliance scoring.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Best-in-market compliance products make audit claims explainable and source-bound. This phase establishes that contract before broader chronology or persisted-event content is added.
- **Risk (1-10):** 4
- **Effort/size:** M
- **Phase-1 slice:** Update only the existing post-finalization PDF and its data contract; no persistence, PDF storage, signature cryptography, deadline math, routing, database schema, or full multilingual PDF redesign.

## L — Persist an append-only cross-workflow audit activity ledger

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Compliance leads and legal advisers reconstructing who did what across Cases, Protocols, Vault evidence, reminders, and exports.
- **Problem:** The current chronology is derived from source records and cannot represent every action, actor, retry, or immutable legal transition.
- **Scope:** Add an append-only event model with actor, event type, source id, case id, timestamp, metadata, retention rules, RLS, idempotency, backfill, and audit UI/export integration.
- **Expected impact:** Creates a durable, cross-workflow audit trail suitable for dispute preparation and portfolio oversight.
- **User-visible outcome:** Users can inspect and export a trustworthy activity history across the complete defect dossier.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** An immutable, explainable audit ledger is a strategic differentiator for regulated construction workflows.
- **Risk (1-10):** 8
- **Effort/size:** L
- **Phase-1 slice:** Decide the event/RLS/retention contract and atomically persist one idempotent `protocol.finalized` event while retaining source-record chronology compatibility.
