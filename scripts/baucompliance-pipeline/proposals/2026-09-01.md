# Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-09-01

## User and market lens

**Primary user/job:** Swiss construction owners, project leads, and compliance administrators need one durable view of every legally relevant date tied to an accepted work, not a calculator result they must re-enter and reconcile manually.

**Workflow moment:** After a Case is created and an acceptance date is known, the user moves between Case maintenance and portfolio deadline review/export. Today the saved Case retains contract/discovery dates only, so its portfolio exposes only an eligible 60-day notice deadline even though the existing calculator already derives acceptance-based two- and five-year milestones.

**Best-in-market outcome:** A Swiss construction-compliance product should make the explicit source date, legal milestone type, and point-in-time calendar export obvious and audit-ready without inferring legal acceptance from a system timestamp.

## S — Explain the legal deadline basis in each work-queue row

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** An owner or collaborator triaging the compliance queue who needs to verify why a Case is urgent without opening it.
- **Problem:** The queue exposes status/countdown but not the governing discovery date and fixed-deadline basis in place.
- **Scope:** Reuse the existing queue timeline to show discovery date plus 60-day-deadline or immediate-notice basis; presentation only.
- **Expected impact:** Faster confidence checks during daily triage.
- **User-visible outcome:** Each row explains the date facts behind its legal urgency.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Explainability improves legal trust, but the queue has received two consecutive substantial investments.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** Small.

## M — Track acceptance-based warranty and limitation milestones on saved Cases

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A construction owner/project lead managing accepted works who needs durable warranty and limitation milestones in the same portfolio as notice deadlines.
- **Problem:** Acceptance is available only in the standalone calculator. Saved Cases cannot retain this explicit source fact, display the resulting two-/five-year milestones, or export them with the portfolio calendar.
- **Scope:** Add a nullable owner-entered `acceptance_date`; expose it in Case create/edit; derive eligible two-year SIA and five-year OR milestones in the existing saved deadline portfolio; identify milestone type in UI and ICS; retain current notice-deadline behavior.
- **Expected impact:** Converts a one-off calculation into a durable, cross-workflow legal deadline register.
- **User-visible outcome:** Cases with an acceptance date show labeled upcoming notice, two-year, and five-year milestones and export each as a distinct calendar event.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Best-in-market compliance software should retain explicit legal source facts and compose all relevant future milestones without manual re-entry.
- **Risk:** 5/10.
- **Effort/size:** Medium.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Nullable date, Case create/edit persistence, read-only portfolio rows, and ICS only. Exclude inferred acceptance, backfill, active notifications, queue reprioritization, and changes to legal formulas.

## L — Governed collaborator review decisions and immutable review history

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A trusted collaborator reviewing a shared queue who must signal review outcomes while the owner retains an accountable history.
- **Problem:** Shared queue phase 1 is intentionally read-only and cannot record a governed review decision.
- **Scope:** Add append-only review events with actor/time and a snapshot of reviewed legal facts, under explicit authority and retention rules.
- **Expected impact:** Establishes accountable multi-party compliance decisions.
- **User-visible outcome:** Collaborators can mark a Case reviewed or requiring owner attention; owners can audit the immutable history.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Accountable multi-party review is strategically differentiating for construction teams.
- **Risk:** 8/10.
- **Effort/size:** Large / strategic.
- **Phase-1 slice:** One append-only decision type over a queue snapshot; no Case/checklist/evidence mutation, assignment, comments, notifications, or external delivery.
