# Daily Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-08-08

## User and market lens

The primary user is a Swiss owner, contractor, project administrator, compliance lead, or adviser who must turn a documented defect into a legally reviewable next action without losing the source facts, deadline context, or audit trail. The highest-friction moment is after a Case contains evidence and notice-source facts but before a defect notice is reviewed: BauCompliance shows readiness data, yet the user must still copy facts into another tool to see what the notice basis will look like. A best-in-market product should make the next legal artifact obvious and source-bound while clearly separating a review draft from legal approval or delivery.

## S — Correct false email-reminder readiness

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A project lead checking whether BauCompliance will actively warn them before a legal deadline.
- **Problem:** The timeline model currently presents email reminder planning as ready even though the product only provides local calendar exports and has no persisted email scheduler or sender.
- **Scope:** Replace the hard-coded positive email-reminder readiness with an honest unavailable/not-planned state and align visible copy/tests.
- **Expected impact:** Removes a legal-trust mismatch and prevents users from relying on a notification that will not arrive.
- **User-visible outcome:** Case readiness explicitly says that no email reminder is planned.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Compliance software must never overstate active deadline protection; truthful readiness is a prerequisite for a future real notification service.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** Small.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Not applicable; this is already the narrow corrective slice.

## M — Source-bound, non-sending defect-notice preview

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A Swiss construction stakeholder reviewing the recipient, service address, defect allegation, and governing Case dates before a defect notice is finalized outside the product.
- **Problem:** The newly merged notice-source contract captures the necessary facts, but Cases still stop at a source-basis card and a manual “draft created” checkbox. Users cannot see a coherent notice draft without copying those facts into Word or email.
- **Scope:** Add a localized, read-only preview on each Case. Enable it only when all three persisted source facts are complete; assemble it only from persisted recipient, address, defect statement, project, canton, contract date, discovery date, and existing notice deadline context; label it draft/not sent/review required; add no PDF, persistence, send action, signature, legal-sufficiency claim, or deadline calculation.
- **Expected impact:** Converts yesterday’s data prerequisite into the first visible legal-artifact phase while retaining human control and source provenance.
- **User-visible outcome:** A user with a complete Case can open an exact, coherent preview and immediately see what requires review before any external action.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** A source-bound review draft bridges evidence, deadlines, and legal action without pretending that automated delivery or legal approval exists.
- **Risk:** 4/10, primarily legal wording, incomplete-state handling, and localization consistency.
- **Effort/size:** Medium, bounded to one page, locale copy, and focused tests.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Read-only in-app preview only; no download, mutation, approval workflow, or delivery.

## L — Shared team Case workspace with four-eyes review

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** Construction project teams coordinating defect evidence, deadlines, protocols, notice review, and responsibility across site management, administration, and advisers.
- **Problem:** Core records are owner-scoped, so teams cannot safely share a live Case or require a second-person review of a legally consequential artifact.
- **Scope:** Organization membership, Case-level access, viewer/editor/approver roles, assignments, immutable approval events, and notifications across Cases, Vault evidence, protocols, and notices.
- **Expected impact:** Enables the marketed team workflow, reduces exported-file handoffs, and adds four-eyes control to legal actions.
- **User-visible outcome:** An owner can invite a verified reviewer to the same Case and require approval before a future notice is finalized.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Multi-party construction compliance needs a shared source of truth and accountable review, not credential sharing or document forwarding.
- **Risk:** 8/10 due to RLS redesign, tenant isolation, invitation lifecycle, evidence permissions, and audit integrity.
- **Effort/size:** Large / 2–4 sprints.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Organization plus Case membership with one verified read-only reviewer; metadata and chronology only, owner-only evidence, edits, deletion, protocol finalization, and notice actions.
