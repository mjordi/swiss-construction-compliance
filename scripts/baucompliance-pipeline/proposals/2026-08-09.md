# Daily Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-08-09

## User and market lens

**Primary user/job:** A Swiss owner, contractor, project administrator, compliance lead, or adviser must turn a documented defect into a reviewable, traceable notice record without losing which facts and deadline context were used.

**Workflow moment:** Immediately after reviewing the new source-bound notice preview and before legal approval or delivery. Today the preview disappears with the page and `noticeDrafted` remains a manual checkbox, so the user cannot prove which revision was prepared.

**Best-in-market outcome:** A construction-compliance product should preserve a factual, immutable revision before approval/delivery, make its provenance obvious, and never imply reminder or delivery capabilities that do not exist.

## S — Correct false email-reminder readiness

- **Target user and JTBD:** A project lead checking whether BauCompliance will actively warn them before a legal deadline.
- **Problem:** The shared timeline contract can report an email reminder as planned although the product has calendar export but no scheduler or email sender.
- **Scope:** Make email-reminder readiness unavailable/false and align visible copy/tests. Do not add notification infrastructure.
- **Expected impact:** Removes a potentially dangerous false assurance.
- **User-visible outcome:** Honest reminder readiness in Cases and derived audit surfaces.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Legal workflow software must distinguish an exported calendar reminder from an actively delivered email.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** S.

## M — Persist immutable source-bound defect-notice draft revisions

- **Target user and JTBD:** A project team member or adviser who needs to preserve exactly what was reviewed before a notice leaves BauCompliance.
- **Problem:** The new complete-source preview is read-only but ephemeral; there is no durable source-bound revision for later review or audit.
- **Scope:** Add an owner-scoped immutable `case_notice_drafts` table. From a complete persisted Case preview, create a timestamped snapshot of recipient, address, defect statement, project, canton, contract/discovery dates, and the existing notice deadline/regime context. Load and show the latest saved revision on the Case. Prevent duplicate submission and leave saved rows read-only to clients.
- **Expected impact:** Converts the notice preview into the first durable legal-record phase and replaces manual copying/screenshots for internal review.
- **User-visible outcome:** A clear “Create draft revision” action and a timestamped latest saved revision that remains stable if the Case is edited later.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Versioned, source-bound notice preparation provides materially stronger audit confidence than a generic template or transient preview.
- **Risk:** 5/10.
- **Effort/size:** M.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Snapshot creation plus latest-revision retrieval only. No editing/deleting saved revisions, PDF, approval, signature, send/delivery, receipt, evidence attachment, or legal-sufficiency claim.

## L — Four-eyes notice approval and traceable delivery lifecycle

- **Target user and JTBD:** Multi-person construction teams and advisers who must jointly review, send, and later prove a legally consequential notice.
- **Problem:** The product lacks tenant roles, reviewer accountability, immutable approvals, delivery status, and receipts.
- **Scope:** Team membership, fixed-revision approve/reject events, delivery-provider integration, receipt/failure tracking, and audit retrieval.
- **Expected impact:** Closes the full loop from source evidence and deadlines to accountable review and provable delivery.
- **User-visible outcome:** A second verified person approves a fixed revision and the exact approved artifact has traceable delivery status and receipt.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** A governed, provable notice lifecycle is a strategic differentiator over calculators, templates, and generic file stores.
- **Risk:** 9/10.
- **Effort/size:** L.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Invite one verified read-only Case reviewer with metadata, chronology, and notice-revision access; record approve/reject events. Keep evidence access, editing, deletion, and external delivery owner-only.
