# Daily Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-07-28

## User and market lens

Primary users are Swiss construction owners, contractors, and project administrators who must turn defect observations into defensible protocols, linked case follow-up, reminders, and handoff-ready evidence. The highest-trust workflow moment today is immediately before protocol finalization: the user is about to create a legal record, but the review surface currently summarizes inputs without making the resulting record, linked-case update, and export availability explicit. A best-in-market product should make those legal workflow consequences obvious before the irreversible action, while eventually preserving them in a durable cross-workflow audit ledger.

## S — Link the dashboard deadline result back to case tracking

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A project administrator who calculated acceptance-based deadlines and wants to continue follow-up in the case ledger.
- **Problem:** The deadline calculator produces useful dates and exports but remains a standalone workflow.
- **Scope:** Add a localized Cases CTA beside deadline result actions, preserving the calculated acceptance date as contextual URL state without auto-creating a case.
- **Expected impact:** Reduces navigation friction between deadline review and tracked follow-up.
- **User-visible outcome:** A clear action from calculated deadlines to the Cases ledger.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Strong products connect legal calculations to operational work rather than ending at a result screen.
- **Risk:** 4/10 — the Cases model does not have a direct acceptance-date field, so the handoff contract could be semantically weak.
- **Effort/size:** Small.

## M — Explain protocol finalization consequences before the legal record is created

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A contractor, owner, or project administrator reviewing a handover/defect protocol before finalization.
- **Problem:** The existing final-review panel confirms project, case, defect, and signature inputs, but does not state what finalization will do: save a finalized protocol, update the linked case’s defect-documentation readiness when applicable, and unlock the PDF artifact.
- **Scope:** Extend the existing step-2 final-review panel with a localized, read-only consequence summary derived from whether a linked case is effectively selected. Add focused tests for linked and standalone protocol paths. Do not alter persistence, PDF schema, checklist semantics, routing, deadline math, or finalization behavior.
- **Expected impact:** Improves legal confidence and prevents users from finalizing under an incorrect assumption about the linked case or resulting artifact.
- **User-visible outcome:** Before clicking Finalize, users can verify the exact records and workflow state the action will produce.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Best-in-market compliance products explain consequential actions before execution and connect each artifact to its audit trail.
- **Risk:** 2/10 — read-only rendering over established finalization behavior.
- **Effort/size:** Medium, bounded phase 1.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Describe only the three already-established consequences on the existing review step; defer history/event persistence and post-finalization activity logging.

## L — Persist an append-only cross-workflow audit activity ledger

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A construction owner, contractor, administrator, or legal adviser reconstructing who completed which legal-compliance action and when.
- **Problem:** Current timelines derive dates and protocol events from source records; checklist, export, evidence, archive, and reminder actions are not preserved as one durable chronological audit trail.
- **Scope:** Define and persist typed immutable events across cases, protocols, Vault evidence, reminders, exports, and lifecycle transitions, with authorization, retention, backfill, and audit UI.
- **Expected impact:** Creates a defensible compliance history and a true multi-workflow audit cockpit.
- **User-visible outcome:** A trustworthy activity ledger showing legal actions, artifacts, actors, and timestamps.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** A durable audit trail is a strategic differentiator for professional construction-compliance software.
- **Risk:** 8/10 — schema, RLS, privacy, idempotency, retention, deletion, and migration decisions.
- **Effort/size:** Large / strategic.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Specify the event contract and persist one idempotent `protocol.finalized` event while retaining source-record-derived chronology compatibility.
