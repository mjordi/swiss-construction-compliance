# Daily Product Improvement Proposals — 2026-08-20

## User and market lens

The primary user is a Swiss construction owner, contractor, project administrator, compliance lead, or adviser responsible for several defect cases. Their job is not merely to store records: before an internal review, insurer/adviser handoff, or management meeting they must see which projects are deadline-critical, which audit steps are incomplete, and whether supporting protocols exist. Today the Vault shows this one card at a time, but the user cannot take a portfolio-wide readiness inventory out of BauCompliance.

A best-in-market Swiss construction-compliance product should turn the legal status, checklist completeness, and source-record links it already knows into an explicit, portable, audit-ready inventory without inventing legal conclusions. It should also preserve source identity and make clear that an export is a point-in-time snapshot, not proof of legal completeness.

## S — Link finalized protocol records back to their Cases

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A project administrator reviewing a finalized protocol who needs to continue work in the linked defect Case.
- **Problem:** The protocol register prints a linked Case ID but provides no direct navigation, forcing a manual trip through the Cases ledger.
- **Scope:** Turn linked Case context into a native link to the matching Cases handoff; leave standalone records as read-only text.
- **Expected impact:** Removes one navigation/search step for linked protocols.
- **User-visible outcome:** A linked protocol card offers a direct “Open Case” action.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Connected compliance records should navigate as a graph rather than dead identifiers.
- **Risk:** 2/10.
- **Effort/size:** Small.

## M — Export a Vault portfolio audit-readiness inventory

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A compliance lead or adviser preparing a portfolio review, insurer/adviser handoff, or internal audit who needs one inventory of active and archived Case readiness.
- **Problem:** The Vault already derives legal status, deadline context, normalized checklist progress, missing audit items, and linked-protocol counts, but these signals are trapped in individual cards. Users must transcribe them manually to review the portfolio outside the product.
- **Scope:** Add a deterministic, localized CSV snapshot built from the owner-scoped Vault data already loaded. Include stable Case ID, project, lifecycle status, legal status/regime and deadline context, normalized completed/total checklist count, translated missing audit items, linked protocol count, and source update timestamp. Export the entire owner-visible portfolio, not the current search/tab subset; identify the generation time and point-in-time/not-legal-completeness boundary. Add guarded client-side download feedback and focused helper/UI/locale tests. Do not add queries, mutations, schema, new legal calculations, evidence contents, notice dispatch details, or active monitoring.
- **Expected impact:** Replaces manual portfolio transcription with a repeatable audit-preparation artifact and exposes readiness gaps across projects.
- **User-visible outcome:** One Vault action downloads a spreadsheet-compatible audit inventory for all loaded projects, with transparent scope and snapshot status.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Best-in-market compliance tools make audit readiness portable and reviewable, while preserving traceability and avoiding unsupported legal claims.
- **Risk:** 3/10.
- **Effort/size:** Medium.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Client-side CSV over existing normalized Vault view models; richer PDF/ZIP evidence dossiers and scheduled reports remain separate.

## L — Governed multi-party compliance work queue

- **Target user and job-to-be-done:** A construction company team that must assign, review, approve, and evidence deadline-sensitive work across Cases, protocols, notices, and the Vault.
- **Problem:** The product is owner-scoped and informative but lacks tenant membership, accountable assignments, four-eyes review, and immutable completion evidence.
- **Scope:** Tenant and role model, reviewer authority, assignment lifecycle, immutable activity events, escalation/reminder delivery, and cross-workflow queue.
- **Expected impact:** Moves BauCompliance from a personal compliance toolkit to an operational team system of record.
- **User-visible outcome:** Teams can see ownership, due work, review status, and evidence of completion in one governed queue.
- **Competitive / best-product rationale:** Enterprise-grade construction compliance requires authority, accountability, and auditability—not only calculators and exports.
- **Risk:** 9/10.
- **Effort/size:** Large / strategic.
- **Phase-1 slice:** Design and implement a tenant-isolated read-only work-item contract derived from existing legal events, only after explicit membership/RLS and authority semantics are approved.
